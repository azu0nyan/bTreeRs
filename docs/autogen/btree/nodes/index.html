Module nodes 

Source
Expand description

Concrete behavior-tree nodes — one type per submodule.

These are the building blocks you compose into a tree:

  • Leaves that look at and act on the context: Predicate, Action, RunCustomFunc, AlwaysSuccess, AlwaysFailure, AlwaysRunning, the timers Wait, WaitTicks and RandomWait, and the navigation leaves SetDestination and GoTo.
  • Decorators that transform a single child: Invert, ForceSuccess, ForceFailure, Repeat, Retry, RepeatUntilSuccess, RepeatUntilFailure, RunLimit, Delay, Cooldown, TimeLimit, Probability.
  • Composites that run a list of children: the sequence family (Sequence, ReactiveSequence, ProgressiveSequence), the fallback / selector family (FallbackSequence, ReactiveFallbackSequence), Parallel, ForEach, and the randomized RandomSequence / RandomSelector / ProbabilitySelector.
  • Conditional branches: IfThenElse (latching) and ReactiveIfThenElse (re-evaluating).
  • The lazy Producer.

Every type here implements BehaviorNode over a context type D. Some bound D on a capability trait (see caps): the timers need HasDelta, the randomized nodes need HasRng, and the navigation leaves need HasNavAgent / FollowPath. They are re-exported at the crate root (and from the prelude), so btree::Sequence and btree::nodes::Sequence name the same type.

A fallback / selector that remembers its progress between ticks.
ForEach
Runs a single child once per item of a collection sourced from the context. Mirrors BTForEach.
ForceFailure
Forces a finished child to Status::Failure. Mirrors ForceFailure.
ForceSuccess
Forces a finished child to Status::Success. Mirrors ForceSuccess.
GoTo
Drives the context’s agent along its navigation path, one step per tick.
IfThenElse
Evaluates a predicate, runs the matching branch, and latches onto that branch until it finishes.
Invert
Inverts a child’s finished result: success becomes failure and vice versa. Status::Running is passed through unchanged. Mirrors Invert.
Parallel
Ticks all of its still-running children every tick, resolving by a pair of ParallelPolicy rules. Mirrors BTParallel.
Predicate
A leaf that succeeds when its predicate is true and fails otherwise.
Probability
Runs its child only with probability p; otherwise fails. Mirrors BTProbability.
ProbabilitySelector
Picks one child at random — weighted by per-child probabilities — and runs it to completion. Mirrors BTProbabilitySelector.
Producer
Lazily produces a child subtree from the context, ticks it to completion, then discards it.
ProgressiveSequence
A sequence that never re-runs a finished child until the whole sequence completes (also known as SequenceStar).
RandomSelector
Like FallbackSequence (logical OR / selector, with memory) but ticks its children in a freshly shuffled order each run. Mirrors BTRandomSelector.
RandomSequence
Like Sequence (logical AND, with memory) but ticks its children in a freshly shuffled order each run. Mirrors BTRandomSequence.
RandomWait
Reports Status::Running for a random duration in [min, max] seconds, then succeeds. Mirrors BTRandomWait.
ReactiveFallbackSequence
A fallback / selector that re-evaluates from the first child on every tick.
ReactiveIfThenElse
Re-evaluates its predicate every tick and runs the matching branch, switching branches (and halting the abandoned one) whenever the predicate flips.
ReactiveSequence
A sequence that re-evaluates from the first child on every tick (“reactive sequence”).
Repeat
Repeats its child until it has succeeded count times, then succeeds.
RepeatUntilFailure
Re-runs its child until the child fails. Mirrors BTRepeatUntilFailure.
RepeatUntilSuccess
Re-runs its child until the child succeeds. Mirrors BTRepeatUntilSuccess.
Retry
Retries its child until it succeeds or has failed count times.
RunCustomFunc
A leaf that runs a plain function fn(&mut D) -> Status and returns its result.
RunLimit
Lets its child finish at most limit times, then fails without ticking it. Mirrors BTRunLimit.
Sequence
A sequence that remembers its progress between ticks (“sequence with memory”).
SetDestination
Asks the context’s navigation agent to compute a path to its desired target, forwarding the agent’s pathfinding Status.
TimeLimit
Fails its child if it runs for longer than limit seconds. Mirrors BTTimeLimit.
Wait
Reports Status::Running until duration seconds have elapsed, then succeeds. Mirrors BTWait.
WaitTicks
Reports Status::Running for a number of ticks, then succeeds. Mirrors BTWaitTicks.
